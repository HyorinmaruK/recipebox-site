RecipeBox

A recipe box for your phone. Offline, in six languages, with the cooking of Korea, Japan, China, Central Asia and Russia at its centre rather than its edges.

Privacy policy Support Source code

Your recipes never leave your device.

There is no account, no analytics and no server. The app makes a network request in exactly one case: when you ask it to import a recipe from a web address. Read the policy.

What it does

  • Over a hundred starter recipes, written in English, Russian, Kazakh, Korean, Japanese and Spanish.
  • Browse by what kind of dish it is, the occasion, what you can eat, or where it is from.
  • Save favourites, scale servings, and get calories and macros worked out from the ingredients — including what it could not count, and why.
  • Import a recipe by sharing a page to the app.
  • A shopping list that merges what you add, and a week planner.
