Something not working, or a recipe that looks wrong?

Report anything here:

Open an issue on GitHub — it is read, and you do not need to know anything technical to write one. Say what you did, what happened, and which language the app was in.

Your recipes are safe

Everything is stored on your device, so nothing is lost if the app has a problem. Before you delete or reinstall, open Settings → Export my recipes and keep the file somewhere. Restore from a backup in the same place puts it all back.

Common things

  • The calorie figure says it did not count everything. That is deliberate. Tap it to see which lines were skipped and why — usually an ingredient with no weight (“a bunch of parsley”) or oil that is poured off rather than eaten.
  • Sharing a page did not import it. Some sites publish no machine-readable recipe. The importer will say so rather than guess, and you can still paste the text in by hand.
  • The starter recipes are in the wrong language. Settings → Language, then Reload in this language. That rewrites the starter recipes only; your own are untouched.
  • A starter recipe has a photo of the wrong dish. Please report it. Every bundled photo is licensed from Wikimedia Commons and credited under Settings → Photo credits.

Languages

The app is written in English, Russian, Kazakh, Korean, Japanese and Spanish. Write in any of them.
